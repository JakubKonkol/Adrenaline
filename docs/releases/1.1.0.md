# Adrenaline 1.1.0

- Added NapiProjekt and Napisy24 alongside OpenSubtitles. Enable providers in Settings; NapiProjekt needs no account, while Napisy24 uses its own API user agent and password. Both Polish providers match the downloaded video by hash.
- Finished movies now reach 100% regardless of missing credentials, failed searches, quotas or subtitle selection. Previously completed jobs with stale progress are repaired at startup. Subtitle retries remain independent of the movie download.
- Added TV torrent filters for 4K, 1080p, 720p, other/unknown quality, source and codec, plus sorting by match, seeders or size.
- Expanded the TV home screen with top-rated films and shows, cinema releases, upcoming films, shows currently on air, action, comedy and science fiction.
- TV downloads now follow title → torrent → subtitle question → subtitle file selection. Pick an OpenSubtitles file before downloading, choose hash matching after download, or skip subtitles. Saved file choices survive app restarts.
- Android TV now offers subtitle synchronization from MENU → Audio & subtitles → Subtitle sync, in 0.25-second steps. Samsung/Tizen already supports the same adjustment under Subtitles → Subtitle settings → Sync; left/right adjusts it and OK resets it. Synchronization applies to subtitle tracks, with text rendered separately from the video; subtitles already burned into the picture cannot be shifted independently.
- Added conversion of Polish time-based TXT and MicroDVD subtitles to SRT for TV playback. Frame-based subtitles use their declared frame rate or the downloaded video's frame rate from FFprobe.

Subtitle services and accounts remain subject to their availability and download limits. Napisy24 requires API access issued by that service, rather than an OpenSubtitles token. You can add or retry subtitles later from the movie's download details on the PC.
